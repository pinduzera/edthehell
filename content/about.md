# This blog

I liked the free internet — in fact I still do, it still exists, and it shouldn't die. Here I want to keep alive that old internet of hobbyists, of passion and of random things.

Platforms are convenient, but they aren't cool, and they're less and less free. Freedom is not negotiable.
